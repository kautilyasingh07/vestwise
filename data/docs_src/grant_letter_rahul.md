# Grant of Stock Options

To: Rahul Verma, Robotics Engineer, Employee ID NR-0217

Dear Rahul,

We are pleased to tell you that the Board of Directors of Nimbus Robotics Private Limited (the "Company") has granted you stock options under the Nimbus Robotics Pvt Ltd Employee Stock Option Plan 2024 (the "Plan"). This letter sets out the terms of your grant. Words with capital letters have the meanings given in the Plan.

## 1. Key Terms of the Grant

| Term | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Grantee | Rahul Verma |
| Number of Options | 2,400 |
| Grant Date | 15 March 2026 |
| Exercise Price | Rs 12 per Share |
| Total vesting period | 48 months |
| Cliff | 12 months |
| Vesting after the Cliff | Monthly, in equal instalments |
| Exercise Window after leaving | 90 days after the Last Working Day |
| Acceleration on acquisition | 50% of Unvested Options vest on a Change of Control |

Each Option gives you the right to buy one equity share of the Company with a face value of Rs 10, at the Exercise Price, once the Option has vested.

## 2. Vesting Schedule

No Options vest before 15 March 2027. On 15 March 2027, the first anniversary of the Grant Date, 600 Options (25%) vest at once. After that, 50 Options vest on the fifteenth day of each month, from 15 April 2027 to 15 March 2030. All 2,400 Options will have vested on 15 March 2030.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 3. Vesting Schedule Summary

| Date | Options vesting | Total vested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before 15 March 2027 | 0 | 0 |
| 15 March 2027 (cliff) | 600 | 600 |
| 15 April 2027 | 50 | 650 |
| 15 March 2028 | 50 | 1,200 |
| 15 March 2029 | 50 | 1,800 |
| 15 March 2030 (fully vested) | 50 | 2,400 |

Vesting continues only while you remain employed by the Company, as set out in clause 4 of the Plan.

## 4. Exercising Your Options

You may exercise Vested Options at any time while employed, up to the tenth anniversary of the Grant Date (15 March 2036), by sending a written exercise notice to the Company Secretary and paying the Exercise Price and any tax the Company must withhold.

## 5. If You Leave the Company

If you leave as a Good Leaver, your Unvested Options lapse on your Last Working Day, and you may exercise your Vested Options within 90 days after your Last Working Day. If you are a Bad Leaver, you forfeit all Vested and Unvested Options. See clauses 6 and 7 of the Plan.

## 6. Acquisition

If there is a Change of Control of the Company, 50% of your Unvested Options will vest immediately before it completes, under clause 8.2 of the Plan.

## 7. Acceptance

This grant is subject to the Plan, which prevails if it conflicts with this letter. Please sign and return a copy of this letter within 30 days to accept the grant.

For Nimbus Robotics Private Limited

Arjun Mehta, Founder and Chief Executive Officer
