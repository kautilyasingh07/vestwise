## Approval of the Employee Stock Option Plan 2024 and the ESOP Pool

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to section 62(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013 and rule 12 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, and subject to the approval of the shareholders by special resolution, the Board hereby approves and adopts the Nimbus Robotics Pvt Ltd Employee Stock Option Plan 2024 (the "Plan") in the form placed before the meeting.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** a pool of 1,000,000 (ten lakh) options, each convertible into one equity share of face value Rs 10, be and is hereby created and reserved for grant to eligible employees under the Plan (the "ESOP Pool").

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the 3,600 options granted under the Nimbus Robotics Employee Stock Option Scheme 2022 shall be rolled into the Plan and counted against the ESOP Pool, on their original terms.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board shall administer the Plan, and that no grant shall be made under the Plan before the date of this resolution.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** Arjun Mehta, Director, and the Company Secretary be and are hereby severally authorised to file the necessary forms with the Registrar of Companies, to convene a general meeting of the shareholders to approve the Plan, and to do all acts necessary to give effect to this resolution.
