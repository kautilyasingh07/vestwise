Dear Vikram,

We are pleased to tell you that the Board of Directors of Nimbus Robotics Private Limited (the "Company") has granted you stock options under the Nimbus Robotics Pvt Ltd Employee Stock Option Plan 2024 (the "Plan"). This letter sets out the terms of your grant. Words with capital letters have the meanings given in the Plan.

## 1. Key Terms of the Grant

| Term | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Grantee | Vikram Nair |
| Number of Options | 2,000 |
| Grant Date | 1 October 2026 |
| Exercise Price | Rs 15 per Share |
| Total vesting period | 48 months |
| Cliff | 6 months |
| Vesting after the Cliff | Monthly, in equal instalments |
| Exercise Window after leaving | 30 days after the Last Working Day |
| Acceleration on acquisition | 50% of Unvested Options vest on a Change of Control |

Each Option gives you the right to buy one equity share of the Company with a face value of Rs 10, at the Exercise Price, once the Option has vested.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 2. Vesting Schedule

No Options vest before 1 April 2027. On 1 April 2027, six months after the Grant Date, 250 Options (12.5%) vest at once. After that, 41 or 42 Options vest on the first day of each month, so that 1/48 of the grant has vested for each full month since the Grant Date. All 2,000 Options will have vested on 1 October 2030.

## 3. If You Leave the Company

If you leave as a Good Leaver, your Unvested Options lapse on your Last Working Day, and you may exercise your Vested Options within 30 days after your Last Working Day. If you are a Bad Leaver, you forfeit all Vested and Unvested Options.

## 4. Acquisition

If there is a Change of Control of the Company, 50% of your Unvested Options will vest immediately before it completes.

## 5. Acceptance

This grant is subject to the Plan, which prevails if it conflicts with this letter. Please sign and return a copy of this letter within 30 days to accept the grant.
